RESEARCH AREAS

What Can You Research in Aerospace?

Aerospace research extends far beyond aircraft and rockets. Researchers work across computing, physics, engineering, biology, materials, robotics and many other disciplines.

01

Aerodynamics

Study how gases flow around aircraft, rockets and other vehicles to improve performance, stability and efficiency.

CFD Wind Tunnels Fluid Mechanics
02

Propulsion

Explore rocket engines, jet propulsion, combustion, electric propulsion and other technologies used to generate thrust.

Rocket Engines Combustion Electric Propulsion
03

Space Systems

Research spacecraft architecture, satellites, communications, power systems and technologies used beyond Earth.

Satellites Spacecraft Mission Systems
04

Autonomy & Robotics

Develop systems that allow aircraft, drones, spacecraft and planetary robots to sense, decide and operate autonomously.

AI Robotics Controls
05

Aerospace Materials

Study lightweight, strong and heat-resistant materials capable of surviving demanding aerospace environments.

Composites Structures Manufacturing
06

Hypersonics

Investigate extremely high-speed flight, where heating, aerodynamics, propulsion and materials interact in complex ways.

High-Speed Flight Thermal Propulsion
07

Human Spaceflight

Research human performance, life support, spacecraft habitability and technologies needed for long-duration missions.

Human Factors Life Support Space Medicine
08

Orbital Mechanics

Study trajectories, orbital transfers, rendezvous, spacecraft motion and mission-design problems.

Orbits Trajectories Mission Design
09

Earth Observation

Use satellites, sensors and remote sensing to study Earth's atmosphere, climate, oceans, land and natural systems.

Remote Sensing Satellites Earth Science
10

Astrobiology & Planetary Science

Investigate planets, moons, planetary environments and the possibility of life beyond Earth.

Planetary Science Astrobiology Exploration
11

Sustainable Aviation

Research quieter, cleaner and more energy-efficient aircraft and propulsion systems for future aviation.

Electric Aircraft Efficiency Sustainability
12

Computational Aerospace

Use simulation, numerical methods, data science and high-performance computing to study complex aerospace systems.

Simulation Computing Data

GETTING STARTED

How Students Usually Get Into Research

01

Find an Area

Start with a subject that genuinely interests you, such as propulsion, autonomy or planetary science.

02

Learn the Basics

Build foundational skills through courses, personal projects, competitions or technical clubs.

03

Find Researchers

Look at university labs, faculty, research centers and government programs working in that area.

04

Apply or Reach Out

Apply to structured programs or contact researchers professionally about possible student involvement.
