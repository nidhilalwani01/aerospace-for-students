RESEARCH AREAS

What Can You Research in Aerospace?

Aerospace research covers far more than aircraft and rockets. Explore some of the major fields shaping the future of flight and space.

01

Aerodynamics

Study how air moves around aircraft, rockets, vehicles and structures to improve efficiency, performance and stability.

CFD • Wind Tunnels • Fluid Mechanics
02

Propulsion

Explore rocket engines, jet propulsion, combustion, electric propulsion and technologies that generate thrust.

Rockets • Jet Engines • Electric Propulsion
03

Space Systems

Research spacecraft architecture, satellites, communications, mission systems and technologies used beyond Earth.

Satellites • Spacecraft • Missions
04

Autonomous Flight

Develop intelligent systems that allow drones, aircraft and spacecraft to navigate and operate with limited human control.

AI • Robotics • Control Systems
05

Aerospace Materials

Study lightweight, heat-resistant and high-strength materials capable of surviving extreme aerospace environments.

Composites • Structures • Manufacturing
06

Hypersonics

Investigate flight at extreme speeds where aerodynamics, heating, propulsion and material behavior become highly complex.

High-Speed Flight • Thermal • Propulsion
07

Human Spaceflight

Research life-support systems, astronaut health, spacecraft habitability and technologies for long-duration missions.

Life Support • Human Factors • Space Medicine
08

Orbital Mechanics

Study spacecraft motion, trajectories, orbital transfers, rendezvous and mission planning.

Trajectories • Orbits • Mission Design
