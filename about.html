ABOUT US

Making Aerospace More Accessible to Students

Aerospace for Students is a nonprofit initiative created to help students understand the opportunities available in aerospace and find clearer pathways into education, research and careers.

WHY WE EXIST

Aerospace Can Feel Difficult to Navigate.

Students interested in aerospace often have to search across dozens of websites just to understand which universities offer strong programs, what competitions they can enter, where internships are available and what careers actually exist within the industry.

Aerospace for Students was created to bring this information together in one accessible place while also providing free guidance for students who need help understanding their options.

Our goal is not to tell students that there is only one correct path into aerospace. Instead, we want to help them discover the many different paths available and make informed decisions about what comes next.

OUR MISSION

Help Students Discover Their Path Into Aerospace.

Our mission is to make aerospace education and career information easier to access by providing students with free resources, guidance and exposure to opportunities across the aerospace sector.

OUR VISION

Opportunity Should Not Depend on Knowing Where to Look.

We envision a future where students from different backgrounds, locations and education levels can discover aerospace opportunities and understand how to pursue them.
